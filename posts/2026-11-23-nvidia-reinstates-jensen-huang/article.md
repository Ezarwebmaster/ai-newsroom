---

Nvidia's board voted unanimously on November 23, 2026 to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive and director, effective immediately.[^1]
The decision reverses the board's stance from days earlier during its third-quarter earnings call, when chair Harvey C. Jones said directors did not intend to bring Huang back and had retained Heidrick & Struggles to search for a permanent replacement.[^3]
Colette Kress, who served as interim chief executive after Huang stepped down on November 12, 2026, continues as chief financial officer while Jones remains board chair.[^1][^4]
Nvidia said directors acted after meetings with Huang satisfied them of his "candor and ability to lead NVIDIA" and after input from senior U.S. government officials convinced them his return "poses no national security risk".[^1]

## White House intervention and ongoing inquiries

President Donald Trump said in a CNBC interview on November 23, 2026 that he had "convinced" Nvidia's directors to bring Huang back, adding that he was "represented in the board meeting" and had spoken to the board directly and indirectly.[^2][^4]
Trump wrote on Truth Social that the U.S. government had no reason to believe Huang's return posed a national security risk, and said Attorney General Todd Blanche confirmed authorities had no evidence Huang engaged in unlawful conduct.[^2][^3][^4]
Huang had resigned on November 12, 2026 after reports and his admission of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman whom U.S. authorities suspect of spying for China, an allegation Beijing denies.[^2][^3][^4]
On November 23, 2026, the U.S. government also added Megaspeed, a Singapore-based firm formerly run by that woman, to a federal trade blacklist over suspected diversion of chips to China.[^2]
Nvidia said an independent board committee investigation into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort "will proceed to conclusion" with his cooperation, and that the company is hiring a Chief Compliance Officer.[^1]
Democratic Senator Elizabeth Warren called the reinstatement "reckless" and said Huang and Nvidia still owe Congress answers ahead of his scheduled December 1, 2026 testimony, while Al Jazeera noted ongoing probes by the Securities and Exchange Commission and the Department of Justice.[^2][^4]

## Market rally and industry reaction

Nvidia's shares jumped 30% at the market open on November 23, 2026 and closed at $165.05, adding $950 billion to the company's market value after falling by a quarter following his November 12, 2026 resignation.[^2][^3]
The rebound lifted broader U.S. equities, with the Nasdaq Composite rising 6.5% and the S&P 500 gaining 4.7%, The Guardian reported.[^2]
In Nvidia's announcement, Huang said he was "honored to return" and pledged to lead "with the greatest humility and renewed commitment", while Jones said bringing him back served stockholders, employees, partners and customers.[^1]
Silicon Valley executives including Elon Musk, OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman and Epic Games chief executive Tim Sweeney welcomed or had lobbied for Huang's return, alongside an open letter that drew more than 180,000 signatures.[^2][^3][^4]
