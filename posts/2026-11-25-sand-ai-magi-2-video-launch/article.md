Sand.ai launched its full MAGI-2 model on November 24, 2026, giving creators up to 30 seconds of video with dialogue, music and sound effects generated alongside the picture.[^1]
The company says the model delivers 1080p video at 25 frames per second and lets the creator specify when a shot should cut.[^1]

## From a character’s wardrobe to the final frame

In one launch demonstration, Sand.ai used reference images to keep a character’s wardrobe consistent across an iris hillside, a rose garden and a valley of lilies.[^1]
As the camera moves between close-ups and wide shots, leaves and petals pass in front of the lens to carry the viewer from one setting to the next.[^1]
A single request can draw on as many as 10 images, five videos and five audio clips, according to Sand.ai.[^1]

Higgsfield’s November 25, 2026 guide describes a simpler way to direct a transition in its web app.[^2]
A creator can supply the opening and closing frames and describe the movement between them, or give the model just the first image to animate.[^2]
The service lets users turn sound off and recommends matching the lighting and setting in the two frames to avoid an abrupt transition.[^2]

## What changed since the August preview

Sand.ai says the August 2026 preview had about 114 billion parameters, of which about 6 billion were active, while the full model has 480 billion total and about 28 billion active.[^1]
The company roughly tripled its pretraining data to 4 trillion tokens, retaining footage with rapid cuts, overlapping sounds and several characters interacting.[^1]
A separate upscaling stage consults the original images again to recover details such as lettering and small faces.[^1]
Sand.ai acknowledges that very fast movement can still break continuity and says the open-source release and detailed technical report will follow.[^1]

On November 25, 2026, MAGI-2 had the highest Elo estimate on Artificial Analysis’s image-to-video leaderboard with audio, based on viewers’ preferences between pairs of clips.[^4]
The site put its rank between first and third, the same range it reported for Dreamina Seedance 2.5.[^4]
On the separate text-to-video leaderboard with audio, Artificial Analysis listed MAGI-2 seventh on November 25, 2026.[^5]

## What half a minute costs

On November 25, 2026, Higgsfield listed a 30-second clip at $2.40 through its API or 75 credits in its web app, equivalent to about $3.69 at the Plus subscription rate.[^2]
