Nvidia board chair Harvey Jones opened the company's quarterly earnings call on November 17, 2026, by saying the board would not rehire Jensen Huang as chief executive and would not take questions on the matter.[^3]
"The Nvidia board does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO," Jones said, according to Decrypt.[^2]
The statement came hours after Huang publicly offered to return if directors believed his reinstatement would benefit shareholders and employees.[^2]
Earlier that day, the board had set up a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, which CBC News described as its first major governance move since Huang's departure.[^1][^3]

## Heidrick & Struggles hired, Kress kept in place

Jones said the board had unanimously accepted Huang's resignation and had hired the executive search firm Heidrick & Struggles to find a permanent CEO.[^2][^3]
<!-- add role="reviser" model="Google Gemini 4 Argon" -->
Jones told the call Huang resigned after the disclosure of "personal behavior inconsistent with the company's code of conduct, standards, and cultural norms", giving a reason after the November 12, 2026, release gave none, CFO Dive and Mint reported.[^6][^7]
He added that the board is weighing internal and external candidates and wants to install a permanent CEO "as quickly as possible, but we won't expedite the CEO before finding exactly the right person", according to CFO Dive.[^6]
<!-- /add -->
Chief financial officer Colette Kress remains interim CEO, and Jones said the board has "every confidence in Colette's leadership, integrity and ability to deliver as interim CEO".[^2]
Kress defended her team on the call: "Our leadership has stood up so strongly over the last week," she said.[^3]
<!-- add role="reviser" model="Google Gemini 4 Argon" -->
She added that customers, suppliers and partners were "all 100% up behind us" and that Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. leadership was in Nvidia's office and "fully engaged in helping us", CFO Dive and CBC News reported.[^3][^6]
<!-- /add -->
According to Decrypt, Jones would not answer questions about the circumstances of Huang's departure or about the independent investigation into his conduct.[^2]

## An offer from Huang, and a campaign the board resisted

Huang said on November 17, 2026, that he would be "honoured" to return if the board deemed it best, CBC News reported.[^3]
<!-- add role="reviser" model="Google Gemini 4 Argon" -->
In that X post, his first statement since resigning, Huang wrote, "I did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at Nvidia, and I have made serious mistakes in my personal life," pledging to cooperate with the board's inquiry, TechRadar and Mint reported.[^4][^7]
<!-- /add -->
Decrypt counted President Donald Trump and Elon Musk among the public supporters of his return.[^2]
<!-- add role="reviser" model="Google Gemini 4 Argon" -->
Mint reported that an open letter on the "Stand With Jensen" website had drawn over 198,000 signatures by early November 18, 2026, including Musk and more than 10,024 self-described Nvidia employees.[^3][^7]
<!-- /add -->
Amazon executive chair Jeff Bezos said at a Semafor conference the same day that without Huang, "I would not hire an outsider".[^3]
<!-- add role="reviser" model="Google Gemini 4 Argon" -->
By contrast, U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called on November 15, 2026, for a "dramatic shake-up" and urged the board to seek an outside successor, CFO Dive reported.[^6]
<!-- /add -->
CBC News reported that Huang resigned on November 12, 2026, after reports of extramarital affairs, and Decrypt reported that one involved a businesswoman whose alleged Chinese intelligence connections have drawn scrutiny from U.S. authorities.[^2][^3]
TechRadar noted that Huang has admitted to an affair, while the spying allegations "remain just that – allegations" with no proof made public.[^4]

## Who sits on the export control committee

Nvidia says the new standing committee is responsible for board-level oversight of its national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management.[^1]
Director Suzanne Nora Johnson chairs it, with directors Stephen C. Neal and John O. Dabiri as members, and it will meet at least quarterly.[^1]
Nvidia's existing national security and trade compliance teams will report to the committee and to Kress, and the committee will retain outside experts.[^1]
In the release, Jones said advanced chips and "the emergence of artificial and superintelligence present unprecedented challenges for national security".[^1]
On the call, he said the committee would improve engagement with U.S. officials as Washington investigates how restricted AI chips reached China, Decrypt reported.[^2]

## Quarterly results and China sales under the leadership cloud

Nvidia reported quarterly revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, with data center sales of $107.6 billion, according to Decrypt.[^2]
<!-- add role="reviser" model="Google Gemini 4 Argon" -->
In its third-quarter fiscal 2027 release for the period ended October 25, 2026, Nvidia said revenue rose 20% sequentially, data center sales grew 110% year over year, edge computing revenue reached $8.2 billion, and GAAP net income reached $65.3 billion, or $2.70 per diluted share, at a 74.8% gross margin.[^5][^7]
Nvidia also said it began shipping Vera Rubin NVL72 systems to CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Nebius, with Kress saying "Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start, our roadmap is on track and our ambition has never been greater."[^5]
<!-- /add -->
It reported $2.5 billion in quarterly revenue from sales to Chinese business units under the Trump administration's limited export permissions, Decrypt added.[^2]
Nvidia forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, and its outlook assumes no data-centre chip sales to China, CBC News reported.[^3]
<!-- add role="reviser" model="Google Gemini 4 Argon" -->
That outlook beat the $125.25 billion LSEG consensus, Kress raised Nvidia's fiscal 2028 revenue growth projection to about 75% from 70%, and shares rose 4.5% in extended trading on November 17, 2026, CBC News and CFO Dive reported.[^3][^6]

## What Nvidia has not disclosed about the inquiry and succession

- Nvidia has not disclosed the scope, timeline or findings of its independent investigation into Huang's conduct, and Jones declined to take questions on the inquiry or his departure.[^2]
- No proof has been made public regarding allegations of a relationship involving Chinese intelligence, which TechRadar noted remain unverified.[^4]
- The board has not named a shortlist of candidates or a target date to appoint a permanent chief executive, saying only that it will not rush the search.[^6]
- Nvidia has also not identified the outside legal, national security and trade compliance experts the new board committee will retain.[^1]
