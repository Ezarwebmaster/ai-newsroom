Anthropic launched the roadshow for its initial public offering on November 10, 2026, marketing 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each.[^1]
It has applied to list them on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT.[^1]
Of those shares, 801,351,354 are offered by Anthropic and 23,648,646 by existing stockholders, whose sales bring the company no proceeds.[^1]
Anthropic expects to give the underwriters, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as lead bookrunners, a 30-day option on up to 123,750,000 more shares.[^1][^6]
At the top of the range, the offering would raise $64.35 billion, TechCrunch calculated.[^3]

## Seven chip and cloud suppliers buy at the IPO price

AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC have agreed to buy $26 billion of non-voting Class N stock in a private placement at the IPO price, TechCrunch reported.[^3]
Google's purchase agreement, filed with the SEC on November 10, 2026, sets its investment at $10 billion, converted into shares at the IPO price.[^2]
The sale is to "take place remotely immediately following the closing of the IPO", once the registration statement is effective.[^2]
TechCrunch reports that Nvidia is also investing $10 billion.[^3]
Counting both deals, Anthropic estimates net proceeds of $84.6 billion to $93.7 billion, TechCrunch reported.[^3]
Global Finance points out that the buyers include chip suppliers and cloud providers with significant commercial relationships with Anthropic.[^6]

## A range below the $2 trillion once discussed

At $78 a share, Anthropic would be valued at about $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis and about $1 trillion without options and similar securities, Reuters reported.[^5]
That is above the $965 billion valuation of its fundraise in May 2026, TechCrunch noted.[^3]
Investors had discussed as much as $2 trillion, but Reuters wrote that "expectations have since come down given Anthropic's substantial infrastructure commitments".[^5]
Terms sent to banks late on November 9, 2026 pointed to a raise of up to $69.3 billion before they were retracted, according to Reuters.[^5]
Jeff Zell of IPO Boutique told Reuters that a valuation below market expectations "allows investors to 'bid up' the IPO", unlike SpaceX's approach.[^5]

## $17 billion of quarterly revenue, $900.9 billion of commitments

The updated prospectus shows third-quarter revenue of $17 billion and $33.2 billion for the first nine months of 2026, TechCrunch reported.[^3]
It also records a nine-month net loss of $104 billion, according to TechCrunch.[^3]
Run-rate revenue reached roughly $81 billion on October 31, 2026, up $10 billion from September 30, Reuters reported.[^5]
Compute and infrastructure commitments total $900.9 billion, against nearly $132 billion in cash and short-term investments.[^3]
Each of the seven co-founders is selling nearly 3.4 million shares, about $250 million each at the $74 midpoint, TechCrunch calculated.[^3]
The founders also hold a Class F share with 50.1% of the voting power, and the Long-Term Benefit Trust elects a board majority, so Class A buyers will have limited influence, TechCrunch noted.[^3]

## Pricing expected on November 18

SiliconANGLE and Global Finance report that the shares are expected to price on November 18, 2026, and Global Finance gives November 19 for the Nasdaq debut.[^4][^6]
TechCrunch reports that up to 20% of the offering is expected to go to retail investors.[^3]
The registration statement filed with the SEC "has not yet become effective", Anthropic said, and no shares can be sold before it does.[^1]
